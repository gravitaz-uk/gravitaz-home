## Challenge

A global network configuration platform was built around web-based data entry, at a volume that had outgrown the interaction model entirely.

## Approach

Gravitaz re-architected the platform from web-based data entry to bulk ingestion with back-end orchestration.

Alongside the technical change, agile practice, pair programming, CI/CD and automated testing were introduced to the India-based engineering teams — the platform change would not have held without the delivery change.

## Outcome

A platform matched to the actual volume of work, and engineering teams equipped with the practice to keep developing it.
