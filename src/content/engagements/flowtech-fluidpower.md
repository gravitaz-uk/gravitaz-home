## Challenge

Legacy ERP and fragmented eCommerce platforms, compounded by major acquisitions, were limiting digital channel growth and slowing feature delivery.

## Approach

An in-depth technical audit, followed by a capability-based As-Is enterprise architecture model in ArchiMate. That model then became the basis for a comprehensive review of technical, process and product capability, against which suppliers could be assessed on the same terms.

An AWS Well-Architected review ran alongside it.

## Outcome

A new eCommerce supplier selected and onboarded against the capability model, and a re-platforming strategy developed for a 30-year-old ERP system. The AWS review identified cost inefficiencies and improved financial visibility.

Also delivered:

- remediated the Pimberly PIM implementation, identifying faults and optimising the data load within the .NET codebase
- defined integration patterns for product, pricing and customer data flows
- introduced DevSecOps practice and cloud-native infrastructure

An architecture practice was established where none had existed.
