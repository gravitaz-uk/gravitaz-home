## Challenge

Salmon's Chief Architect resigned following the company's acquisition by Wunderman Thompson, leaving the Net-a-Porter account without architectural leadership at a point of organisational uncertainty.

## Approach

Gravitaz stepped in as Interim Managing Architect, leading four domain architects across infrastructure, content, commerce and integration.

## Outcome

Architectural continuity and delivery momentum maintained through the transition to new Salmon leadership — an interim engagement in the most literal sense, holding a function together until it could be handed on.
