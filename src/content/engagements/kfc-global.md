## Challenge

Omnichannel retail and B2C commerce across six global markets — Australia and New Zealand, Thailand, Japan, India, Canada and South Africa.

Fragmented architecture, ad-hoc feature intake and inconsistent delivery practice were slowing time-to-market and undermining scalability and cost efficiency across all six.

## Approach

As part of the Foundry senior leadership team, Gravitaz helped switch suppliers to a more effective near-shore delivery model and form a new KFC Global engineering team.

Heading the architecture and design practice meant directing cross-functional teams — engineering leads, solution architects, QA and delivery managers — through a Kanban model, applying a quality-property-driven approach across B2C engagement channels, integration, the BFF and service layer, DevSecOps and the SaaS commerce back end.

Governance was decision-record-based and fed into delivery through a biweekly scrum-of-scrums, so architectural decisions reached the teams that had to implement them.

## Outcome

A stable, scalable omnichannel platform, reducing operational risk and accelerating global feature rollout.

The key objective was delivering against a committed feature roadmap over the final six months — something the KFC markets had not previously experienced. Reusable frameworks and architectural practice were established that sustained delivery across multiple international markets after handover.
