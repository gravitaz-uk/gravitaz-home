## Challenge

Which? needed to deliver consumer-facing digital services at a pace its existing software development lifecycle could not support.

## Approach

Gravitaz introduced Which? to agile and CI/CD, and transformed the SDLC into a working practice rather than an adopted vocabulary — a distinction that decides whether this kind of change survives the engagement.

## Outcome

Delivery led across Reviews, Price Comparison, Mortgage Calculation and Cars, on an established agile practice that continued after handover. This was a long engagement by the standards of the practice, and a hands-on one throughout.
