## Challenge

Review the process for evaluating defined benefit pension scheme liability across decades of legacy applications. A machine-learning investment was under consideration.

## Approach

Gravitaz assessed the issues as arising from poor underlying data quality rather than from the evaluation process itself — a distinction that determined whether the proposed investment could work at all.

## Outcome

Advised the CEO and CTO that the data could not effectively train a machine-learning model, and recommended investing in the underlying IT tools and services instead.

The recommendation prevented a costly investment in the wrong solution. Saying no to a proposal already in motion is often where an independent architect earns the fee.
