## Challenge

A new generation of mobile and web front ends needed back-end services that had not been designed for that kind of consumption, and the cost of each integration was being paid repeatedly.

## Approach

Gravitaz defined the target-state architecture for API-led integration and service orchestration, introducing governance and capability maps so that integration became a managed capability rather than a per-project negotiation.

## Outcome

Reduced friction in integrating back-end services into the new front-end estate, with a capability model giving the organisation a shared view of what it already had.
