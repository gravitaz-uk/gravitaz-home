## Challenge

Design a B2B liquidations portal to recover value from substandard product output, using a House of Brands approach so that the core brand was protected from the discounted channel.

## Approach

Working with the CIO to understand the business model and the existing SAP-based production and warehouse automation estate, Gravitaz produced As-Is application, data and technology views, then reduced the architecture to a vendor-neutral set of capabilities, services and value streams.

Vendor-neutrality mattered here: the estate's future depended on choices that had not yet been made, and modelling in supplier terms would have foreclosed them.

## Outcome

The full challenge set was catalogued — the near-term sale of Silver Quality stock, and the longer-term cost of the SAP automation suite. A Mindera engineering team was then engaged to deliver the solution together with an ongoing support model for a local operations team.
