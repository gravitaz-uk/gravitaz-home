## Challenge

A catalogue of failures had led the executive team to seek outside advice. The presenting problem was a list of incidents; the question was what connected them.

Gravitaz assessed the issues as symptoms of the transition from start-up to scale-up, caused by absent technical governance — demand management, change control, incident handling and service assurance.

## Approach

Technology, people and process were reviewed together rather than separately, because the failures crossed all three.

Quality characteristics were then agreed, aligned and assigned to stakeholders as recognised concerns, so that each gap had an owner rather than a recommendation. This is the difference between a report that gets read and one that gets acted on.

## Outcome

An architectural assessment covering technology maturity, cloud-native stance, security, quality and organisational structure, recommending:

- an IT operations capability focused on stability and service assurance
- a DevSecOps uplift in both practice and tooling
- a change-management governance process bridging technology and business teams
