## Challenge

Support dynamic assembly of curated peer-reviewed article collections, and monetise access across audiences with very different purchasing models.

## Approach and outcome

Gravitaz re-architected the publishing workflow for dynamic assembly of curated collections, and designed a paywall and entitlement model supporting individual, institutional IP-range and hybrid authorisation.

Entitlement is the harder half of an academic paywall: the same article is legitimately reachable by several routes, and the model has to hold all of them without leaking access.
