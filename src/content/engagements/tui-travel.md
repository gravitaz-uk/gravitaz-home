## Challenge

Establish the target architecture for omnichannel travel, in a domain where the product itself is unusually hard to model — perishable, temporal, and assembled from supplier inventory.

## Approach

Gravitaz established API-first integration as the target, consolidated the design teams, and introduced governance aligning product, engineering and business.

The domain problems solved along the way included supplier contract management, flight segment mapping, B2B and B2C identity, a temporal product catalogue (CPQ), yield management, reservations, fulfilment and faceted search.

## Outcome

The next-generation SAP Hybris website was delivered on time, on a new design-first delivery practice.
