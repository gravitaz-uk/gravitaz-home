## Challenge

Espalier Ventures was considering the acquisition of a US-based SaaS platform and needed an independent technical view before committing.

## Approach

Gravitaz provided technical due diligence on the platform, and produced a reference target architecture showing how the capability would integrate with the existing estate — so the question was not only whether the technology was sound, but whether it would fit.

## Outcome

The acquisition proceeded on the strength of the assessment, and the service was adopted by INTO.

*The engagement was curtailed by the Covid-19 lockdown.*
