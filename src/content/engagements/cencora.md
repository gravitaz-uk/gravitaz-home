## Challenge

Following a series of mergers and acquisitions, the Cencora International Business Group had no standard pattern for B2C omnichannel and unified eCommerce across its European online pharmacies. Each market was running a supplier-led programme on its own terms.

## Approach

Working with the group's Director of Enterprise Architecture, Gravitaz reduced the supplier-led programmes of work to a common capability set, then established composable architecture patterns markets could adopt without re-litigating the technology choice each time.

The patterns were proven in delivery rather than on paper: close work with Alliance Healthcare Norway secured approval for, and then managed delivery of, the key technical enablers for the eCommerce and customer satisfaction workstreams.

## Outcome

B2C and omnichannel composable patterns delivered and approved, then applied to Boots Norway and Pharmavance in France. A standards-compliant route was agreed by adapting supplier-led work rather than replacing it, which avoided unplanned rework and the political cost that comes with it.

## Build and integration

**Built:** BFF and unified commerce service layer as TypeScript microservices on Kubernetes.

**Bought and integrated:** Adobe Commerce, Shopify, BigCommerce, Sanity CMS, Algolia, PIMCore, ServeFirst SaaS for customer engagement, and Azure Integration Fabric.

## Stakeholders

Represented the International Business Group to the US enterprise architecture governance team, and engaged suppliers, Norway market stakeholders and the global senior leadership team.
