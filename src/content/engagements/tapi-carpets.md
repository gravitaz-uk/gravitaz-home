## Challenge

As a retail challenger scaling fast, Tapi's early-stage architecture could not support eCommerce growth, ERP integration or scalable customer engagement.

## Approach

Gravitaz designed and implemented the target architecture for B2B and B2C commerce, integrating ERP, CRM and eCommerce, with patterns for data, identity and cloud-native operations.

This was a design-authority engagement as much as a design one: hands-on authority to delivery teams, and help establishing DevOps practice. Stakeholder engagement ran to the owners, CEO, CPO, COO and CFO.

## Outcome

Tapi scaled from start-up to nationwide retailer on an integrated omnichannel platform, and an ERP transformation programme was established to move from Navision to Microsoft Dynamics 365.
