## Challenge

Define the target enterprise architecture for large-scale online assessment and publishing, where the compliance requirements are as demanding as the scale.

## Approach

Organisational structures and solution contexts were modelled in ArchiMate via Orbus iServer, giving a shared reference for a landscape spanning assessment, publishing and delivery.

## Outcome

Two pieces of work outlasted the engagement:

- **EPAM (External Party Access Management)** — a blueprint for anonymised, compliant online testing, later adopted at Cambridge University Press
- **SELTS** — a secure digital IELTS testing platform for UK visa compliance, used by the British Council
