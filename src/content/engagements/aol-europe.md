## Challenge

Two decisions with long consequences: how inbound call-centre operations should decide what to offer a customer, and which content management platform the business should commit to.

## Approach and outcome

Gravitaz defined the next-best-action strategy for inbound call-centre operations, evaluating a Pegasystems upgrade against adoption of Chordiant.

The CMS RFI/RFP was led separately, evaluating Vignette against CoreMedia — an early instance of the supplier-selection-against-a-capability-model approach that has run through the practice since.
