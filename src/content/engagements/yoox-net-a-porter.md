## Challenge

YNAP needed to expand omnichannel capability while meeting GDPR, with problems in customer identity management, data compliance and integration across its three core eCommerce platforms.

## Approach

Gravitaz provided solution architecture leadership to modernise the commerce stack, and designed and delivered the integration of the Gigya CIAM platform (now SAP Customer Data Cloud) across all three platforms, enabling unified authentication, consent management and secure data handling.

Domain responsibility for privacy and compliance sat within the cybersecurity team, extending Gigya with a PKCE exchange implemented through CDK-deployed Lambda functions in AWS.

## Governance

Chaired cross-team architecture calls between London and Bologna, and owned the architecture decision log spanning customer onboarding, CRM and marketing (Salesforce), security, merchandising, cart and content integration, order processing, payments, fulfilment and warehouse operations.

## Outcome

GDPR compliance achieved through a centralised CIAM solution, customer identity and consent unified across platforms, and the foundation laid for international growth and brand integration. The enterprise architecture governance function was strengthened through the domain architect role for privacy and compliance.

**Context:** AWS, Gigya, GraphQL, security, privacy and compliance.
